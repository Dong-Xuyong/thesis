[https://doi.org/10.1049/ell2.12683](https://doi.org/10.1049/ell2.12683)

## Application in the paper

Unsupervised anomaly monitoring of large-scale wireless base stations using KPI multivariate time series.

## Reported quantified results

China Mobile WBS results: precision 0.910, recall 1.000, F1 0.953; area F1 scores 0.962, 0.965, 0.931; total F1 boosted by up to 9%.

## Why useful for this literature review

Shows how detection can generalise across many grouped telecom entities.

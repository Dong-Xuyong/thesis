## Application in the paper

Unsupervised anomaly retrieval in high-dimensional financial transaction time-series subspaces.

## Reported quantified results

Real dataset: 70GB, 600M+ transactions, 415 MCCs, 16 trials. Greedy MAP 0.86 in 1345.50s; evolutionary MAP 0.72 in 87.62s. Production scale: 22 min greedy vs 1.5 min evolutionary for one month/600M records.

## Why useful for this literature review

Very relevant: anomalies may appear only in selected subspaces, similar to selected telecom aggregations.

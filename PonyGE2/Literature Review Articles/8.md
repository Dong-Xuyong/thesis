## APA citation

Oğuz, H. T., & Kalaycıoğlu, A. (2022). Anomaly detection in multi-tiered cellular networks using LSTM and 1D CNN. *EURASIP Journal on Wireless Communications and Networking, 2022*(1). [https://doi.org/10.1186/s13638-022-02183-7](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13638-022-02183-7)

## Application in the paper

Femtocell outage/anomaly classification in multi-tier cellular/self-organising networks.

## Reported quantified results

At 2 dB shadowing and 30 m ROI: 1D CNN accuracy 80.1%, LSTM 79.08%. Best-case true positive rate >95% and overall accuracy >80% for four FAP states.

## Why useful for this literature review

Shows that cellular anomaly detection depends on tier/group context.

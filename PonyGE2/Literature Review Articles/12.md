## Application in the paper

Hierarchical KPI forecasting, anomaly detection, and root-cause drill-down in enterprise indicators.

## Reported quantified results

Hierarchy: 1 top-level, 2 middle-level, 74 bottom-level series. LightGBM MAPE: count 3.51%, amount 4.95%, beating Lasso 4.49%/6.05% and XGBoost 3.85%/5.33%.

## Why useful for this literature review

## APA citation

Mefleh, A., Debicki, M. P., Mubarak, A., Saade, M., & Weill, N. (2025). Beyond the norm: Unsupervised anomaly detection in telecommunications with Mahalanobis distance. *Computers, 14*(12), Article 561. [https://doi.org/10.3390/computers14120561](https://doi.org/10.3390/computers14120561)

## Application in the paper

Unsupervised Mahalanobis anomaly detection for telecom KPIs, aggregated from cells to sectors/sites, with SHAP contributor analysis.

## Reported quantified results

AUC improved from 0.77 (raw KPI) to 0.86 (with denominators), 0.93 (adjusted KPIs + denominator), and 0.99 (learned adjustment + denominator). Large benchmark MD AUC 0.86 in 1.87s; One-Class SVM took 4h34m15s.

## Why useful for this literature review

Strong example that aggregation and KPI representation change anomaly-detection quality.

## Key results to cite

AUC: 0.77 (raw KPI) → 0.86 (denominators) → 0.93 (adjusted + denominator) → 0.99 (learned adjustment). MD AUC 0.86 in 1.87s vs One-Class SVM 4h34m15s.

## What to put in your literature review

Lead grouped section. Representation/aggregation dominates scorer choice. Strongest telecom evidence that changing the view changes detection quality. Frame gap: they hand-design aggregation; you search it.

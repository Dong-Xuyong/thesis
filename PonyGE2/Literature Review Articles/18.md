## Application in the paper

GA/SEISEN feature selection for multivariate time-series forecasting.

## Reported quantified results

Electricity MAE/features: 207.193/7704 without selection vs 194.400/3813 with SEISEN. Traffic: 0.008/20688 vs 0.007/10316.

## Why useful for this literature review

Shows evolutionary search can reduce time-series input size while preserving or improving performance.

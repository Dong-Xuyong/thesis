## Application in the paper

Large-scale exact motif/join discovery, especially seismology-scale repeated patterns.

## Reported quantified results

Exact motifs over 100,000,000 subsequences and 499,999,999,500,000,000 pairwise distances. Brute force estimated 1,585 years; GPU-STOMP completed in 12 days.

## Why useful for this literature review

Shows Matrix Profile methods can scale when many candidate time series need scoring.

## Key results to cite

100M subsequences; brute force ~1,585 years vs GPU-STOMP 12 days.

## What to put in your literature review

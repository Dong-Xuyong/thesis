## APA citation

Burgueño, J., de-la-Bandera, I., Mendoza, J., Palacios, D., Morillas, C., & Barco, R. (2020). Online anomaly detection system for mobile networks. *Sensors, 20*(24), Article 7232. [https://doi.org/10.3390/s20247232](https://doi.org/10.3390/s20247232)

## Application in the paper

Real-time anomaly detection/tracking over LTE/5G network KPIs.

## Reported quantified results

24,725 LTE-A cells, 45 days, 650 measurements/cell at 15-min intervals, 240 hourly KPIs. 380 labelled cells, 15 KPIs, mean 3.8% anomalous samples. Detected about 25% more total anomalies and almost 80% of all network anomalies.

## Why useful for this literature review

Useful operational benchmark for telecom monitoring with limited labels.

## Key results to cite

24,725 LTE-A cells, 45 days, 650 measurements/cell, 15-min intervals, 240 hourly KPIs. 380 labelled cells, 15 KPIs, 3.8% anomalous samples. ~25% more anomalies detected; ~80% of network anomalies captured.

## What to put in your literature review

Emphasise operational scale and label sparsity. KPI-selection bottleneck: only 15 of 240 KPIs monitored — manual coverage is impractical. Supports need for automated view/series selection.

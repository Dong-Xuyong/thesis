## APA citation

García González, G., Martinez Tagliafico, S., Fernández, A., Gómez, G., Acuña, J., Mariño, C., & Casas, P. (2022). Mining multivariate time-series for anomaly detection in mobile networks: On the usage of variational auto encoders and dilated convolutions. In *Proceedings of the 8th SIGKDD International Workshop on Mining and Learning from Time Series*.

## Application in the paper

Detects anomalies in multivariate monitoring data from an operational mobile ISP/TELCO network.

## Reported quantified results

TELCO DC-VAE: recall 67%, precision 47%, F1 52%; S-VAE baseline: recall 38%, precision 48%, F1 33%. Median DC-VAE F1 59% vs S-VAE 31%.

## Why useful for this literature review

Useful comparator for deep multivariate telecom anomaly detection.

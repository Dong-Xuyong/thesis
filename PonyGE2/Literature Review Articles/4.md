[https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309093](https://doi.org/10.1371/journal.pone.0309093)

## Application in the paper

Detects and localises urban events using mobile network signalling data.

## Reported quantified results

Paris/Orange data: about 27M daily subscribers, about 12 TB/day, 3 months, 1-min cell-level aggregation, 86 ground-truth events. Best precision 28.63%; Notre-Dame fire alarm within 5 minutes of visible smoke.

## Why useful for this literature review

Shows abnormal real-world events can be detected from aggregated telecom activity counts.

## Key results to cite

Paris/Orange: ~27M daily subscribers, ~12 TB/day, 3 months, 1-min cell aggregation, 86 ground-truth events. Best precision 28.63%. Notre-Dame fire alarm within 5 minutes of visible smoke.

## What to put in your literature review

Use for aggregation visibility, not fault-detection benchmarking. Abnormal real-world events appear after the right aggregation. Low precision shows the difficulty even when signal exists. Bridge to grouped time-series section.

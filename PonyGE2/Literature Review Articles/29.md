## APA citation

Van Onsem, M., Ledoux, V., Mélange, W., Dreesen, D., & Van Hoecke, S. (2023). Variable length motif discovery in time series data. *IEEE Access, 11*, 73754–73766. [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3295995](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2023.3295995)

## Application in the paper

Matrix Profile–based variable-length **motif** discovery on synthetic data, Numenta NYC taxi counts, and 25 Skyline production KPI series from telecom-provider customers.

## Reported quantified results

About 3–8× faster than VALMOD. Example Skyline series: motifs of 288 points (~1 day) and 12 points (~1 hour) found in one distance-matrix pass. No detection F1/AUC (manual motif verification).

## Why useful for this literature review

Published Matrix Profile application on telecommunications operational KPIs. Task is motifs, not discord scoring or aggregation search.

## Key results to cite

Telecom KPI motifs via MP (Skyline, 25 series). 3–8× vs VALMOD. Dual-length 288 vs 12 points.

## What to put in your literature review

In §3.4: MP has been used on telecom KPIs for motif discovery; it still does not search which aggregation to score with discords.

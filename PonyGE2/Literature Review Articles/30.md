## Application in the paper

Genetic algorithm at three levels for multivariate time-series anomaly detection: (1) evolve feature subspaces (which input series are modelled together), (2) evolve an autoencoder architecture per subspace, (3) non-gradient fine-tuning of weights. Final model is a bagging ensemble. Fitness: weighted validation MSE loss (subspace/model levels); number of false positives (fine-tuning). Benchmarks: SWaT (51 series), WADI (123), MSL, SMAP.

## Reported quantified results

Point-wise F1 (Table 2): SWaT 0.82, WADI 0.62, MSL 0.57, SMAP 0.77, mean 0.70 — best of 14 methods (next best mean: UAE 0.54). SMAP +0.18 over NASA LSTM. Ablation: improved subspace/model evolution +0.01–0.15 F1; fine-tuning a further +0.01–0.10. Cost: 18.8–131.8 h per dataset on 8 GPUs.

## Why useful for this literature review

Evolutionary search choosing *which time series to combine* for anomaly detection — the AD counterpart to Linares-Barrera (forecasting) and Contreras (classification).

## Key results to cite

GA over subspaces + architectures + weights; mean point-wise F1 0.70 vs 0.54; 14 methods; 4 sensor benchmarks.

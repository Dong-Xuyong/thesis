## Application in the paper

Exact time-series joins for motifs, discords/anomalies, shapelets, segmentation, and density estimation.

## Reported quantified results

Example join reduced from 153.8 days to 1.2 hours. STOMP gave 4.7x speedup on about 2M-length series. Cloud run reduced 4.2 days to 14.1 hours. GPU-STAMP reported 30x speedup over STAMP.

## Why useful for this literature review

Supports Matrix Profile as a practical anomaly/discord scoring method for many candidate series.

## Application in the paper

Online anomaly detection and localisation over hierarchical ISP operational logs.

## Reported quantified results

> 94% accuracy locating anomalies; found >94% of anomalies from ISP reference method. ADA gave 14.2x lower runtime, 2x lower memory, <1% time-series error, and 99.7% anomaly-identification accuracy.
